Why it works in stages

Lovorian isn’t a menu of services — it’s a sequence. Each pillar builds on the one before it, because that’s how trust actually works between a business and the person touching its systems. The early stages use only public information; the later stages, which touch your internal data and workflows, come only after I’ve proven the work. Most businesses begin at Pillar 1 — the $500 Digital Presence Audit — and decide what comes next from there.

The Five Pillars

Pillar 1

Digital Presence Audit & Remediation

Public-data-only phase

Where every path starts, and the lowest-risk place to begin. I look only at what’s already public — how customers and AI find you today, scored across search, AI answers, and local presence. No access to your systems or data required.

From there I fix what the audit surfaces, in-house, at a flat fee sized to your situation, then close with a wrap-up call and a clean signoff.

The audit: $500, delivered within two business days.

Remediation is flat-fee, never hourly, and scoped to exactly what the audit finds — from a light Foundation cleanup to a full Turnaround for messier, multi-platform situations. You get one firm, fixed number before any work begins, quoted after I’ve seen what your audit turns up — no hourly surprises, no scope creep.

Value: a diagnosed, fixed public presence · Access: public data only

Pillar 2

Knowledge: Data Structuring & Governance

First phase that touches your internal data

The first time I ask for access beyond what’s public — and only because you’ve now seen the work. I clean up messy or outdated information, set clear rules for who and what can reach it, and organize your files so AI answers from your real business instead of guessing.

Value: clean, AI-ready data · Access: internal data, under governance you control
Pillar 3

Empowerment: Custom AI Agents

Built for how you actually work

With your data in order, I build task-specific AI agents around your actual workflows — never on autopilot. Every agent is human-in-the-loop: it does the work, you approve the decisions that matter. That includes a ready-made scheduling agent for customer bookings, plus custom agents built for whatever your business needs most.

Value: AI agents built for your workflow, with you in control · Access: your workflows, human-in-the-loop
Pillar 4

Command: Monitoring & Control

Visibility and control

Once agents are working for you, you get a single dashboard to see and govern everything they’re doing — what’s running, what it’s touching, and the time and value it’s creating. No black box: you can always watch, and always step in.

Value: one place to control and watch the AI · Access: oversight layer across your agents
Pillar 5

Scale: Continuous Optimization

Ongoing advisory

The path doesn’t end at "operational." As your business changes, I stay on in a low-commitment advisory role to keep your AI decisions grounded and your ROI climbing — adjusting as new tools, needs, and opportunities show up.

Value: a system that keeps growing with your business · Access: ongoing partnership
